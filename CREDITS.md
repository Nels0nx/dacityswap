## Notes

- Product thumbnails in `apps/web/public/thumbs`, and the Telegram stickers built by
  `scripts/telegram-pack.mjs`, are renders of these models and carry the same licences.
- Several vehicles are colourways: the same file shown with a different body paint, applied at
  run time. They are listed as separate products but are not separate models.
- All models were compressed with glTF Transform (meshopt geometry, 1K WebP textures). That is
  an adaptation; for the CC BY models, this file is the required attribution and notice of change.
- The Khronos sample models are stand-ins. Several carry the Khronos logo, which is a trademark
  and is not covered by the Creative Commons licence. Replace them with original models before a
  public launch. `scripts/meshy-generate.mjs` holds prompts for the intended catalogue.
- The backdrop photo is a low-resolution public-domain image, shown deliberately out of focus.
  For launch, replace it with a licensed high-resolution photograph.
- `supercar.glb` and `superbike.glb` closely follow real production vehicles, and the bike's
  fairing carries lettering that resembles a manufacturer's name. Real vehicle designs and
  marks belong to their makers. Restyle or replace both before a public launch.
- No artwork, screenshots, models or logos from any game or game publisher are used.
