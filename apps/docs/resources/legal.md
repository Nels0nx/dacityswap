# Legal

The legal position of the project, stated plainly. This page is a summary for readers, not
legal advice.

## Independence

DacitySwap is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by
Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or any other game publisher. Game titles appear only to
identify what a listing relates to. All trademarks belong to their owners. No artwork, logo or
other asset from any game is used on this site or in the product.

## Status

DacitySwap has not launched. The company is being set up, and trading opens once the legal and
licensing work is complete. Until then, nothing on this site is an offer to buy, sell or hold
anything for anyone.

## Real-money trading and publishers' terms

Many publishers' terms of service forbid selling in-game items or currency for real money, and
at least one publisher is actively litigating against a marketplace of this kind. A marketplace
does not become lawful by being well built.

DacitySwap will not open a market for a game unless there is a legal basis for it: a
publisher's permission, a game whose terms allow such trade, or a model that an independent
legal opinion confirms. Obtaining that opinion is the first item on the
[roadmap](/resources/roadmap), ahead of any engineering.

No game has been confirmed as a launch market. In particular, nothing here should be read as a
claim that any current or upcoming title permits real-money trading.

## Payments and regulation

Holding money for others and settling it between them is regulated activity. At launch:

- card payments will be held and paid out by a licensed payment provider, not by DacitySwap;
- handling crypto-assets for users in the EU requires authorisation under MiCA, and the
  stablecoin flow will not go live without the appropriate basis;
- sellers will be verified, and anti-money-laundering and tax-reporting duties will apply.

## $DCSWAP and the fee policy

DacitySwap's policy is that marketplace fees are not kept as company revenue: once the
marketplace is live, the service fee and the commission are used to buy $DCSWAP on the open
market. It is a policy of the company, not enforced by the escrow contract. It says what the
fees are spent on and nothing about the price of $DCSWAP. Nothing on this site is an offer of
$DCSWAP, investment advice or financial advice.
